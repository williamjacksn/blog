title: Beaten by Cheesecake
urlname: cheesecake
date: 2005-04-02T00:24

Do you remember how I dominated the tortilla world? Unfortunately, I do not have that effect on all foods everywhere.
Specifically, I have a twisted love/hate relationship with cheesecake from The Cheesecake Factory.

Today is my best friend&#x02bc;s birthday. Last night he celebrated with a small gathering of friends at The Cheesecake
Factory. There were nine of us, and when we were seated we let the server know we would only be having dessert.

>
> Life is uncertain. Eat dessert first.
>

She still brought us bread and butter. That&#x02bc;s not a bad thing, because the bread is _so_ good, but I probably ate
too much, knowing what lay before me. When my slice of Fresh Banana Cream Cheesecake came out, I was still incredibly
optimistic about the whole situation.

After a fair amount of bites, including a taste of my best friend&#x02bc;s Godiva chocolate, I realized that polishing
off my piece of cheesecake would increase the chances of my stomach exploding violently by several dozen percent.

For cheesecake, I was willing to take that chance.

I cleaned my plate, then slouched down in my chair, using my _huge_napkin as a blanket of sorts (for comic relief). If I
had seams, I felt like I would burst at them any moment.

I really don&#x02bc;t mind being beaten by cheesecake every once and again.

In closing, happy birthday!
