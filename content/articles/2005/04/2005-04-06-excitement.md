Swimming gives me time to think. Today, in the pool, I was thinking about what I needed to do later this week. I was
trying to remember when my exam for Computer Science was, so I could be prepared. I suddenly remembered that my Computer
Science exam was held last night.

That&#x02bc;s right, I completely forgot about it, and rearranged the furniture in my apartment instead.

As I write this I am waiting for a response from my professor regarding whether I can take a makeup exam or not. If you
hear a knocking, that&#x02bc;s the sound of my head hitting the wall repeatedly for the next hour.
