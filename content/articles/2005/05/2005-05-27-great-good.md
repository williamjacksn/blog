What with school being over, and my employment terminated, I have been wasting time watching movies. These are the films

* [_Star Wars: Episode III - Revenge of the Sith_](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0121766/)

  I&#x02bc;ve seen this one before, but it was a while ago so I wanted to remind myself. It&#x02bc;s sad, like most
  World War II films are, but the music is really good.
