Gutenberg is a visual theme I developed for Wordpress. I wrote the code all by myself, but there was a bit of
inspiration from a LiveJournal style called &ldquo;A Novel Conundrum&rdquo;.

It&#x02bc;s very basic. Black text on white background, one column, very much like the old printed page. My favorite
part is the little diddle image that separates sections.

[Download Gutenberg 0.3]({static}/images/2006-02-12-gutenberg-0.3.zip)

I thought Gutenberg was how I wanted my site to look, but one day I realized that I like colors way too much.
